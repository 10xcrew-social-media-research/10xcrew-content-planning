01

How I will work with Linda

Our focus is Instagram Reels and Stories, YouTube Shorts and useful longer videos. We decide together which ideas fit Linda's upcoming content and the time available.

What I can help with

  • Research topics and turn them into video ideas and simple shot lists.
  • Write captions, help film and edit, and prepare subtitles and covers.
  • Help schedule and publish the posts assigned to me.

What we do together

  • Choose priorities from Linda's existing planning.
  • Divide the work and review the content.
  • Discuss results and decide what to improve.

Colleagues can contribute expertise or appear in content when agreed. Linda approves content before publication.

02

Instagram and YouTube are the main focus

Instagram can show the people and daily work at 10XCREW. YouTube can explain the team's expertise through useful videos. We adapt each idea to its audience.

Instagram

Help people get to know the crew.

For potential colleagues and brand teams interested in the people behind 10XCREW.

  • Reels: Meet the Crew, office life, training days and short tips.
  • Stories: moments from a shoot or event, with a simple question for viewers.
  • Visual posts: a few photos or clear slides when they tell the story well.

Use @10xcrew_agency. Agree the caption, cover and posting date with Linda.

YouTube

Help viewers understand one useful topic.

For marketplace managers, e-commerce teams and brands looking for practical answers.

  • Shorts: one question, one answer and an example.
  • Longer videos: explain a case or topic that needs more context.
  • Existing material: turn a useful moment from a talk or interview into a clear clip.

Choose a title that says what viewers will learn. Plan a clear cover for longer videos and fit publication into Linda's schedule.

LinkedIn supports this work: share a relevant takeaway or selected clip on the company page when it fits Linda's planning. TikTok stays on hold.

03

Give each video a clear opening

A hook is the first line or opening shot. Show the topic immediately, then deliver the answer or story promised by the opening.

A practical question

“bol or Amazon first for a Dutch brand?”

For a YouTube Short or explainer. Follow with one useful answer.

A result from our work

Open with one supported result from a client case, then explain what changed.

For a YouTube case explanation. Show the result clearly and include the source and period.

A person and their expertise

Introduce a colleague through a question they help clients solve.

For an Instagram Meet the Crew Reel. Show what their work involves.

Inside a working day

“A day with Pacvue at our Eindhoven office.”

For an Instagram Reel or Stories about a training day or event.

A timely change

Explain what an upcoming platform update or event means for a brand.

For a timely YouTube explanation or short Instagram tip. Check the date before posting.

One question, one minute

Use a short video to explain one practical point from the team's knowledge.

For a YouTube Short and an adapted Instagram Reel. Keep each version focused.

05

Build on Linda's existing planning

The topics, dates and posting frequency come from the existing plan. We agree where I can help and how to divide each task.

  1. Check the plan

    Look at upcoming Instagram and YouTube content together. Agree which pieces need help and who prepares, reviews and publishes them.

  2. Prepare content

    Divide the scripting, filming and editing. Prepare a shot list and gather any facts, existing footage or input we need.

  3. Review together

    Check the message, facts, sound, subtitles and branding. Watch the video on a phone, make changes and get Linda's approval.

  4. Publish

    The agreed person schedules or posts the content on the right 10XCREW account.

  5. Learn from it

    Review how long people watch and how they respond. Use what we learn to improve the next videos.

06

Make the most of each shoot

One interview or office shoot can give us material for both channels. We choose the useful pieces and edit them for the purpose of each video.

Before filming
Agree the topic, people, shot list and intended formats with Linda. Divide the filming and editing tasks.
Capture material
Record a clear answer and supporting shots of the person or their work. Check the sound and permission to use the footage.
For Instagram
Make a Reel that introduces the person, shows the activity or shares a quick tip. Use suitable moments from the shoot for Stories.
For YouTube
Edit a Short or fuller explanation that makes sense on its own. Give it a clear title and a focused opening.
07

Show our people and keep the branding consistent

Formats we can reuse

  • Meet the Crew: introduce a colleague and their work.
  • Crew at Work: show how the team approaches a task.
  • Event Days and Crew on Location: share useful moments from events.
  • Welcome to the Crew: introduce new colleagues.
  • Crew Moments: share a natural, light moment from the office.

Keep the look familiar

Use the existing 10XCREW templates, colours and typography. Keep text easy to read and use real people and places.

  • Petrol
  • Teal
  • Bright teal
  • Orange
  • White

Use vertical clips for Reels and Shorts, add subtitles and agree permission with everyone shown.

From the GoSpooky research: adapt people stories, team life and event moments into Instagram Reels and Stories. For YouTube, turn useful knowledge from the team or an event into a focused explanation.

08

Review Instagram and YouTube results together

Use the accounts' analytics with Linda to see which topics and formats are worth developing. Start by saving the results of the videos we make.

Instagram

  • Attention: how long do people watch the Reel?
  • Response: which videos get useful comments, saves or shares?
  • Stories: which moments lead to replies or questions?

Use this to discuss the opening shot, pace, topic and caption.

YouTube

  • Attention: where do viewers keep watching or leave?
  • Shorts: how often do people choose to watch rather than swipe away?
  • Response: which videos prompt useful comments or bring subscribers?

Use this to discuss the opening, explanation, title and future topics.

How we compare the results

Compare Reels with Reels and Shorts with Shorts, over similar periods after posting. Agree realistic goals with Linda using the accounts' own starting results.

Metric guides: Instagram Reel watch time and YouTube Shorts analytics.
