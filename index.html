Expert content with Emile Valkestijn

All 171 articles on 10xcrew.com carry the author name “10XCREW team”. This format gives our expertise a face.

The Marketplace Question

Working title

What
Every two weeks Emile answers one question that marketplace and e-commerce teams ask, with a number or an example from our own work.
Where
A video of 60 to 90 seconds on his own LinkedIn profile, reposted by the page, and cut down for a Short and a Reel.
Start
7 October, four episodes in eight weeks
His time
About 100 minutes a month

In an earlier analysis of 222 competitor posts, posts that named a person had a median of 21.5 reactions against 9. A small public sample, so no proof of cause and effect.

Emile's median after eight weeks

  • 15 or moreContinue and add Henrike Colijn, if his episodes also beat their paired company posts in at least three of the four weeks.
  • 7 to 14Keep Emile, change the topic or the video format, and run another eight weeks.
  • 6 or fewerStop, or try a different face.
